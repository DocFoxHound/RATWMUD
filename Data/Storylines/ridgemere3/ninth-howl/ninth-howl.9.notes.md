# The Ninth Howl, Chapter 9: The Moot Night (as built, 2026-11-24, branch `r3-nh-live`)

`ninth-howl.9.json`, the last chapter of Act III "The Moot Night" (`campaign.approve` with `ninth-howl.7` and `.8`; Ch 8
starts it with `story.start {audience: same, carry: true, when: ended}`). campaign.md Chapter 9 (9.1 to 9.8), decisions
§6, doc 86 §9.3. Check: `python3 tools/nh_check.py Data/Storylines/ridgemere3/ninth-howl/ninth-howl.9.json`. The program
is generated by `/tmp/nhlive/ch9/gen.py` with its rehearsals in `/tmp/nhlive/ch9/tests9.py` (outside the tree, as the
wave J scripts were).

## Shape
- Audience everyone (30 days, cap 5,000), `activeDays` 18, prefix `nh`. 64 variables (the cap), 35 campaign triggers,
  10 phases, 5 quests, 8 scenes, 14 regions.
- `ch9_run_up` (the working phase). Its `ready` block: participation over `ch9_did` of the active wolves (30%, floor 2,
  cap 150), or the soft maximum of 36 game days with `ch9_auto` on; `minDays` 12 since the chapter; the bar when
  `ch9_wait_bar` is on; `auto: true`, `slowAfterDays` 36, `slowNeedsYou: true`. Exit `ready` to `ch9_called`.
- `ch9_called`: the call. The Moot's name by the season on the call's first hour (`calendar is` winter: `nh_moot` and the
  realm key `midwinter`, else `nine_howls`), on every board; a push noted in `nh_pushed` (`ch9`) when participation or
  the minimum time was not met; the bar short at the call is the worse road (scar `moot_short`, and both arms start
  carrying one silent step instead of two); the Captain's band gathers at the Pass (`story.area.spawn`, camp `nh_pass`)
  and Ulric walks there. Exit on `day begins` to `ch9_eve`. A call made in a day's last hour (an Advance at 23:xx) would
  meet midnight before its hour (the game writes `day begins` before `hour`), so `call_late` makes the call on that
  `day begins` before the exit; the night is then the coming dusk.
- `ch9_eve`: at dusk the keepers walk to their posts and the Moot's cast to Market Square; the Turn plays if two of its
  three keys hold (parley offered, Fenwick confessed, the ledger shown or kept) and Ulric is at the Pass; the herald's
  dusk notice (by the Moot's name); C10 `foe.add` champion "the Grey Captain" (`nh_captain`, band 6, or 8 if the ford
  burned, like `cty_stone_sunton`) and the stranger Hakon (like `rm_palle_bilgemarsh`); then `ch9_night`. The posts and
  the ballot stay open until this dusk (the keepers' choices are laid again in each open phase).
- `ch9_night`: the Holding Howl, one window a game hour (§9.2): hour 1 the Pass and Saltwell, 2 the Upper Mere and
  Southmeadow, 3 Coldwater and Fenshore, 4 the Glasswater relay (the trunk takes the reach of the better arm) and
  Mirebridge, 5 the Oakhaven road and Larkfield, 6 Oakhaven, 7 the waystation, 8 the Bay Light; then the counting and
  `ch9_moot`. A post answers if the howl can reach it (`reach_*` at least 1) and a wolf stands in its region, or its
  raised town's keeper does, or a wolf who kept it stands anywhere in its town (the Pass also when the Captain is
  turned or beaten). An answer sets the arm's reach to 2 (3 when strong: two wolves there, a post Ch 4 practised,
  `<post>-strong` in `nh_posts`, or the Pass turned), a silence takes one off.
- `ch9_moot`: the scene `nh9_moot` (Brenna, Gerrit, Ambrose, Gorm; the square's `choice`); its choice, or its end with
  none, or its failure, or (`moot_late`) nothing heard from it 0.1 game day on, runs the result: the most voices wins unless the top two are within one voice (Regency).
- `end_ottilie`, `end_lamp`, `end_regency`, `end_bought`: the ending scene, `nh_crown` (keep, 90 days), the figure
  (`nh_ruler` Ottilie "Queen of Ridgemere" or Dagfinn "the Lamp-King"; `nh_lord` Brenna "Speaker of the Moot";
  `nh_villain` Ambrose "the Lord Protector"), eight boards and a banner; an hour later `ch9_epilogue`.
- `ch9_epilogue`: the Moot Roll (a journal note and the deed by the Moot's name), Howl-friend of each town (the top
  ceil(pledged/10) by voice, 3 at most: the title, the Keeper's `npc.greet`, 10 pennies from the treasury under the
  counters or bread in kind), Voice of the Realm (max(1, active/25)), the Lantern (the hero figure `nh_hero` with the
  wolf, and the title), the Protector's Own (Bought Crown, `p_marks` 3 or more), the Concord's score and verdict
  ("Howler of the Holding" for every wolf whose post quest is done), the beaten Captain's judgement by ending on all
  eight boards, the lasting talk, the forty holds let go (`npc.unprotect`, `npc.unspare`), `nh_stage` forgotten
  (cast-and-talk 4.12); then, a game day later (`epi_end`), `finish` (success): deeds the game gives only to a wolf in the
  world (`deed.award`, `item.give`) are held for a wolf away until they are next here (`ifRefused: when here`), and the day
  gives them time to come. Ch 9 starts no chapter: the campaign ends here.
- **Refusals.** Nothing on the night may pause the run: every walk, howl, scene, stranger, foe, camp, greeting and title
  carries `ifRefused: skip` (a keeper dead or still walking, a foe already gone with his band, a scene's cast busy).

## Tasks (participation, `ch9_did`)
- **Kept a post**: the keeper's choice "I'll stand <post> with you on the Moot night." (`npc.offer keep_<post>`, the
  reply their Q say from cast-and-talk §3.21) starts the journal quest `keep_post` (one post a wolf); lamp oil and a horn
  or a handbell given to that keeper finish it (10 points; a region post joins `nh_posts`; the keeper's own thanks from
  cast-and-talk 3.21 as `npc.greet`, in their voice). A post quest put aside frees the wolf to ask another keeper.
- **Carried lanterns for the drowned** (`lanterns`, closure side): a journal offer at the Mirebridge crossroads at night;
  one lantern a night with lamp oil, three nights (`lantern_t`); the optional part sends the Fenshore innkeeper to the
  crossroads with her letter, and the scene `nh9_nell` plays when she gets there (`scene arrived`).
- **Escorted a stage of the bell's road** (`bell_road`, closure side): unless the scar `bell_lost`, the bell moves a stage
  an hour while a wolf stands with it (Upper Mere, Coldwater, Glasswater, the waystation, the Bay Light); a camp
  `nh_bell_road` forms at the next stage past a threat of 50 or a sold Watch; at the Bay Light Solveig rings it as the
  howl comes in (scene `nh9_bell`, `nh_bell=rung` kept).
- **Raised a silent voice** (`late_howl`, closure side): a Keeper's "Howl it with me" (the tree's `nh_chorus`) starts it,
  and the Keeper howls (`npc.howl`) for the wolf to follow, so a lone wolf can do it (campaign 5.1); a chorus of two or
  more in a town not yet in `nh_raised` raises it (board, banner, `nh_voice=raised` and `nh_howl=found` for the town)
  and completes the quest for every wolf there. A country town's green is out of doors in the realm's own region
  (Greenholt, the Sallow Fens...), so the game writes a chorus there as `wild`: the town is read from the chorus's town,
  or its tile inside the town's square region; Mirebridge is no town at all and goes by its crossroads region alone.
- **Answered the Envoy**: the tree's `nh_gm_q_honest|proud|evade` at Gorm; an honest answer of the first three adds 1 to
  the Concord.
- The door quest `door` (journal, on arrival): ask the herald the Moot rule (`nh9_rule`) and pledge (the tree's
  `nh_pledge`); a side story's share (6). Pledges and backings are the tree's story-heard `nh_pledge` / `nh_back`; each
  can change once in Ch 9 (`ch9_changed`), the backing at a cost of 2 of the wolf's voice. The Keepers' `nh_keeper=on` is
  remembered for whoever takes the pledges (the Keeper, or the fallback if dead; Oakhaven's by `nh_b_logger`).

## The counting (9.4), in half-voices
- Each town's verdict: the pledged wolves' (`p_howl`) voice by backing (`tally`), times two, plus one for its lean
  (Coldwater Blood if Oda contests, Larkfield Purse if the ledger was kept, Fenshore Lamp if he stands and the lights did
  not fade, Frosthollow Purse if the seam was crooked or Lamp if Fenwick confessed, Oakhaven never Purse if the hills
  were lost); a tie the lean does not settle goes to the Moot. 2 if the town's post answered, else 1. Before the night the
  same counting posts each town's verdict so far on its board every day.
- Howl-friends: the top max(1, a tenth) of the town's pledged wolves, 3 at most; the thanks are the words of whoever
  takes the town's pledges now (the Keeper, else the fallback; Oakhaven's by Ulla's name), with a thin-purse variant
  when the pay is bread.
- Houses: Greatly Purse (Moot if the ledger was shown; abstains if kept); Fell Purse (Moot with Gudrun's match and the
  hills kept); Grayrock Moot (Lamp, or Blood if he does not stand, with the crown and the wharf names; Purse if absent);
  Timberland Moot; Fell and Timberland abstain after the `fell_timber` quarrel.
- The free wolves: the top backing of every wolf's voice gets 2 voices; the square's choice 2 with the writ, 1 without.
  Bought: min(3, `nh_bought`/5). A short Moot (fewer than 5 towns heard, scar `writ_lost`): 2 more to the Council's side.
  If Dagfinn does not stand, the Lamp's voices go to the Moot.
- Concord (9.6): the posts answered, Tansy (+2, +1, -1), peace kept, ford held, the Envoy walked, the honest answers;
  -1 each for a broken truce, a Watch sold, and `moot_short`. 14 or more answers, 9 to 13 holds, else bends.

## What differs from campaign.md, and why
- **The keepers' post quests are one journal quest**, `keep_post`, started by thirteen keeper choices (the giver budget
  stays 2: pledge and post), rather than thirteen quests (the chapter's 14-quest budget, and one post a wolf).
- **The 64-variable cap**: the nine `nh_voice_*` strands and the Ember thirds are not read in Ch 9 (raising a voice is
  the late howl's chorus); no `nh_surplus`, `nh_quick_registered` (the Concord's +1 for a registered Quickened) or
  `nh_b_willa` (Willa keeps Glasswater if alive, else Harrow). Scars stand in for `nh_b_ford`, `nh_b_writ`, `nh_b_bell`,
  `nh_b_hills`, `nh_b_lights`, `nh_b_peace`.
- **Dagfinn's standing**: `nh_b_stands` from Ch 8; when Ch 8 never tallied the Question (`nh_t_stand` and `nh_t_step`
  both 0, e.g. an act published without it) the call decides it from the wolves' own `p_advice`, and sets `nh_dagfinn`.
- Howls are counted by presence at each window (the fallback of 9.2 rule 1), not J5 `howled`; a resident keeper's
  `npc.howl` is sent when the post answers. The Southmeadow stone override, Greatly's
  double vote for the Crown's loan, Mirebridge's silent half cast by the Council, buy-backs in Ch 9 and the town-store
  purses for the Howl-friends (paid from the treasury instead) are not built.
- The ruler's post swap (C14 `npc.post`) is not used: `dm.story_posts` (migration 0080) is not on Ridgemere 3. The crown
  is a figure and talk (the sequel line of 9.5).
- `nh_howl` is written `ran` (kept) when Ridgemere's waystation answered; the library reads no `night` or `broke`.
  `nh_moot` is kept 90 days (the library's after-the-Moot lines read it).
- A post is strong with two wolves there, not max(2, helpers/25): `count in region` takes a fixed number.

## Rehearsals (6, all pass; nothing uncovered; each ends a game day after the epilogue opens)
1. One active wolf (the floor asks for all of one): the Pass alone answers, the bar short (scar), the Blood (Queen
   Ottilie), the Captain flees, the realm bends.
2. Five active wolves: not ready with one, ready with two; five posts kept and answered; the Captain beaten at the Pass
   (the Keep, the judgement); the Regency (the Moot scene ends with no choice); the realm holds.
3. Two hundred active wolves: 59 not enough, the sixtieth makes it; the daily verdicts; six towns raised; every post
   answers; the Bought Crown and the Protector's Own; the realm answers.
4. A winter call (the Midwinter Moot): the door quest, a pledge and a backing, the bell's whole road and its ring, the
   lanterns and the letter (Nell at the crossroads), three late howls (two on a green, written `wild`, one led by the
   Keeper), a post quest put aside and another taken, the Blaze; Dagfinn stands by the wolves' advice; the Turn lets the
   Captain go; the Lamp-King.
5. Advance anyway before the minimum time (`nh_pushed`); no quarter at the Pass; the Moot scene fails (the verdict all
   the same).
6. The soft maximum with `ch9_auto` off: still not ready at 36.5 game days; the admin's Advance anyway at the alert, in
   a day's last hour (midnight before the call's hour: `call_late`); a night with nobody out, the Moot scene never
   reporting (`moot_late`), the Regency, the Captain fled.

**Not rehearsable today:** `ch9_auto` on and `ch9_wait_bar` on (a case cannot set a story switch, and a trigger cannot
hear `dm signal`), the Turn's own keys and the other earlier chapters' branches (a case cannot preset world variables:
it starts from the declarations). The Turn's three outcomes are rehearsed by its scene choice. An engine follow-up: a
rehearsal case key for the story's and the world's first values.
