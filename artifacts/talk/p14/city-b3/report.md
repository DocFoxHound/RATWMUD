## Voices

- **rm414, the brewer's paired stars.** This wolf pairs stars into debtors and creditors and turns a clean cup upside down when the last account comes square.
- **rm415, the camp ember in town.** This wolf brings a lingering smell of hill smoke to town and listens for camp quiet between the bustle of hooves.
- **rm416, the pocket provisions.** This wolf whittles provisions for a shop small enough to fit inside a drawer and lets the contents of a sack speak before attending to the mark of the owner.
- **rm417, the uneven column.** This wolf marks a doubtful column with a pressed leaf, gives a puzzle the courtesy of a second reading and maintains a perfectly composed silence until the figures are ready to speak.
- **rm418, the hot cup timer.** This wolf puts a small copper on an empty warm saucer to gauge a serving walk and wants a stall where every cup arrives worth drinking.
- **rm419, the soot print astronomer.** This wolf presses a smoky thumb to scrap paper and discovers separate night skies in the resulting specks.
- **rm420, the coal-stained glove.** This wolf keeps a lucky coal in an immaculate fold of linen and has a dry word ready when a grand doorstep invites inspection.
- **rm421, the wooden pantry.** This wolf whittles tiny wooden provisions, keeps the pantry door shut against drizzle, and says little while finding room on the shelf for an extra supper.
- **rm422, the cellar-mouth star.** This wolf looks for one patch of sky above a cellar mouth and insists on stating a fair figure just once.
- **rm423, the chalk stall.** This wolf chalks a market stall around an empty crate, measuring every grand promise of space by the simpler question of whether two laden wolves can pass.
- **rm424, the coal-map stall.** This wolf marks a different pitch for a future market stall on each old wrapper, carries the marking coal for luck and lets nobody see all the maps together.
- **rm425, the die by the bedpost.** This wolf balances a die on the bedpost before a storm and lets its first little tap carry the blame for a restless night.
- **rm426, the fourth story folded shut.** This wolf cuts three notches for three oft-told stories and keeps the unspoken fourth wrapped flat against the chest.
- **rm427, the pantry facsimile.** This wolf keeps a clean second copy at home and counts progress in answers, never in the number of stairs climbed to request one.
- **rm428, the spoon star.** This wolf takes the final stair in two eager strides to catch a first star in the curve of a clean spoon and treats a late meal with the same care as a grand dinner.
- **rm429, the stair garden.** This wolf follows a shaft of sun up the stair with a small pot of earth and is more willing to wait for a slow seed than an idle promise.
- **rm430, the hood with two brims.** This wolf turns an oilcloth brim to shelter a second muzzle and listens carefully to what a traveller says under cover.
- **rm431, the board of charcoal accounts.** This wolf sets charcoal men on a little board to remember unsettled accounts, keeps a square clear for an old wharf loss and has very little patience for a wolf who confuses soot with poor work.
- **rm432, the listening smith.** This wolf judges small household jobs by ear and has no patience for noise that does nothing useful.
- **rm433, the star on the whetstone.** This wolf turns a whetstone under one steady star and keeps both the provision knife and the words of a letter in good order.
- **rm434, the unmarked draughtsmen.** This wolf rehearses arrivals in a hall with plain draughtsmen and likes to know how a welcome works before adding family marks to a seating plan.
- **rm435, the singing jar lids.** This wolf taps sea tunes on larder lids, requires a proper price before passing a jar, and chooses an evening fishing place beyond the elbows of the crowd.
- **rm436, the seed-row harbour.** This wolf marks unbroken seed rows with ship-shaped pegs and lets the apparent order conceal a private plan.
- **rm437, smoke above ruled water.** This wolf measures a swimming distance in the blank edge of a working slip, feeds the used paper to a clean fire and notices at once if anyone has been rearranging the desk.
- **rm438, the pocket passenger.** This wolf wraps pocket coal in two layers of cloth, calls the smallest piece a passenger, and likes to hear where other feet are going.
- **rm439, the drying-line clerk.** This wolf rules a pencil line on the sill to settle arguments about the rain, tidies a desk by working off its contents, and insists that even a riddle must give an honest account of the question.
- **rm440, the iron's bright corner.** This wolf files one bright corner on an iron sample, sets a casting of dice beside the figures, and expects a clear answer about the weight, the route and the return.
- **rm441, the dark drawer.** This wolf keeps a drawer in such order that its contents can be found in darkness, guards private business carefully, and rates a dry towel above most luxuries.
- **rm442, the doorway soot seal.** This wolf leaves a private soot mark inside a finished errand list and carries a clean cloth for every surface that belongs to somebody else.
- **rm443, summer light on account.** This wolf pencils the reach of summer light beside the day's last entry and gives a damp cough a succession of implausibly official descriptions.
- **rm444, the eaves arithmetic.** This wolf calculates with chalk, listens to rain for the missing beat and keeps an uneasy reckoning out of the class margin.
- **rm445, the singing hood.** This wolf turns one corner of an oilcloth hood back to hear a song through the rain and keeps the other ear comfortably out of the weather.
- **rm446, the walker's second copy.** This wolf chooses the long road and the short answer, keeps a second fold of paper inside a coat, and likes tea to be as hot on arrival as it was meant to be.
- **rm447, the caller above the watermark.** This wolf can project the price of a button above a passing wagon, and has the patience to sort a tray by touch while the wagon rolls past.
