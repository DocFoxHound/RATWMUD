# One entry a line; '#' starts a comment. An entry is refused only as a whole word (and with a plural: s, or es after
# s, x, z, ch or sh), so place names and ordinary words that happen to hold one pass (Scunthorpe, Essex, Penistone,
# Cockburn, spices). A leading '*' refuses it inside longer words too: only for words no ordinary English word
# contains, or whose ordinary words are listed with a leading '!' (let through: snigger). An irregular plural is an
# entry of its own.
# Look-alikes (0 1 | 3 4 5 7 @ $), repeated letters, Cyrillic and Greek look-alike letters, accents and invisible
# characters are all read before matching, so list the plain spelling only.
