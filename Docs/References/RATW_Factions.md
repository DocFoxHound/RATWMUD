# The factions of Upper Accord, Ridgemere and Ser Ferro

Drafted 2026-10-03 for Docs/Design/32-parties-chapters-factions.md, Part 4 ("fill in the factions"), following the
setting bible's Faction Template (§25). **This is a proposal, for the Dungeon Master to adopt, change or reject.**
None of it is in the live tables until a DM puts it there.

The game reads a faction's kind, named members with their ranks, and relations from `live.factions`,
`live.faction_members` and `live.faction_relations`. Everyone working in a place a faction claims counts as its
member, and its guards and traders count as its officials. Suggested starting relations are given as
*disposition / stance*, read as "how this faction regards that one".

**What every faction gives and takes from a Chapter by band** (doc 32, 4.3–4.4):
- *Known well:* rentals, first-tier missions, fair prices.
- *Trusted:* building on its land, second-tier missions, its quartermaster's gear, and members' word heard by its Watch.
- *Sworn:* fortifying on its land, third-tier missions, escorts, and recognition as a minor House.
- *Distrusted:* a quarter more at its stalls.
- *Hostile:* turned away.
- *Enemy / war:* shown red.

The entries below add what is particular to each.

## Ser Ferro

### The Crown of Ser Ferro
- **Kind:** city (government)
- **Territory:** the palace terrace, the Hall of Petitions, the city walls and the Marches' farmland by right
- **Leadership:** King Aurelio di Castellane, Queen Livia; the Chancellor Ottavia Lanza runs the day to day
- **Public purpose:** order, the King's justice, the river trade's safety
- **Actual purpose:** keep the six palazzi balanced against each other and the Concord's vote in the Crown's pocket
- **Methods:** petitions and favours, the Guard, licences and tolls, marriages
- **Rivals:** the Syndicate (−60 / hostile); wary of the Concord (−10 / tense)
- **Allies:** the Church of the Iron Saint (55 / friendly), the Ser Ferro Guard (80 / allied), the Merchants' Guild
  (30 / friendly)
- **Offers:** a royal licence to build in the Marches (Sworn), a Crown seal opening the Hall of Petitions, and in time
  a minor House's title
- **Takes away:** licences, the right to rent within the walls, and audiences
- **Missions:** carry petitions to the Marches' villages, escort grain barges on the river, guard the Hall of
  Petitions on audience days
- **Visual:** white and silver, a crowned river

### The Church of the Iron Saint
- **Kind:** npc (religion)
- **Territory:** the Cathedral Rise, the Chapter House, the Clergy House, the House of Saint Chiara
- **Leadership:** the Prelate of the Cathedral; the Chapter of canons
- **Public purpose:** the Bright One's order, charity, the crypt of the Kings
- **Actual purpose:** keep the Concord's religious authority in Ser Ferro and its archives closed to outsiders
- **Methods:** confession, alms, the archives, sermons that move the lower town
- **Rivals:** the Syndicate (−50 / hostile); uneasy with the Warden Order over Quickened wolves (−5 / neutral)
- **Allies:** the Crown (55 / friendly), the Concord (70 / allied)
- **Offers:** sanctuary in its halls, healing at Saint Chiara's (Trusted), and blessings that make the Crown look kindly
- **Takes away:** sanctuary, burial in consecrated ground, and the lower town's goodwill
- **Missions:** carry alms to the wharf tenements, fetch records from Upper Accord, keep vigil in the crypt
- **Visual:** iron grey and white, an anvil under a star

### The Ser Ferro Guard
- **Kind:** city (military, the Watch)
- **Territory:** the City Guard Barracks, the gates, the palace guardhouse, every street on its rounds
- **Leadership:** the Captain of the Guard, answering to the Royal Hand
- **Public purpose:** the King's peace
- **Actual purpose:** the same, with a captain who knows which palazzo's sons to look away from
- **Methods:** patrols, the gaol, fines, informers in the wharf
- **Rivals:** the Syndicate (−70 / hostile)
- **Allies:** the Crown (85 / allied)
- **Offers:** a member's report weighs more (Trusted), escorts on the river road, and a blind eye to old fines (Sworn)
- **Takes away:** entry at the gates after dark, its patience
- **Missions:** keep watch at a gate, carry a warrant to a village constable, deliver a prisoner's meals
- **Visual:** red tabards with a white tower

### The Merchants' Guild
- **Kind:** guild (commercial)
- **Territory:** the Merchants' Guildhall on the Heights, the Wool Guild Hall, the market square's stalls
- **Leadership:** the Guildmaster, elected from the palazzi's trading families
- **Public purpose:** fair weights, honest prices, the river trade
- **Actual purpose:** keep the trade in the families' hands and newcomers paying dues
- **Methods:** stall licences, credit, price agreements
- **Rivals:** the Syndicate in its respectable clothes (−30 / tense)
- **Allies:** the Crown (35 / friendly), Ridgemere's Council of Houses (20 / friendly)
- **Offers:** better prices at Guild stalls (Trusted), warehouse space to rent, and credit (Sworn)
- **Takes away:** the right to sell in the market, and credit
- **Missions:** deliver goods to the wharf warehouses, carry a letter of credit to Ridgemere, keep a stall for a
  morning
- **Visual:** gold and green, a set of scales

### The Syndicate
- **Kind:** npc (criminal)
- **Territory:** the wharf tenements, the Muddy Oar's back room, warehouses no one admits to owning
- **Leadership:** unknown to most; it acts through the Couriers and proxy gangs
- **Public purpose:** none, officially it doesn't exist
- **Actual purpose:** the river's smuggling, the city's debts, and whichever officials it owns
- **Methods:** proxies, blackmail, bribery, fences, the Couriers
- **Rivals:** the Crown (−60), the Guard (−80 / hostile), the Church (−40 / hostile)
- **Allies:** none it would admit to
- **Offers:** fenced goods, quiet passage, and names (Trusted), with escape from a warrant at a price (Sworn)
- **Takes away:** whatever it likes, from whoever crosses it
- **Missions:** carry a parcel without opening it, keep watch on a warehouse door, deliver a "gift" to a clerk
- **Note:** helping it ripples against the Crown, the Guard and the Church. A Chapter that swears to it can't swear to
  them.
- **Visual:** none worn openly; a knot tied a certain way

## Ridgemere

### The Council of Houses
- **Kind:** city (government)
- **Territory:** the Rain Hall, the Magistrate's Court, the Hall of Tallies, the old city within the walls
- **Leadership:** the five Houses' heads in council; the Chancellor and the magistrate carry out its will
- **Public purpose:** govern Ridgemere for all
- **Actual purpose:** govern Ridgemere for the Houses; keep the Sump working and quiet
- **Methods:** ordinances, tallies and taxes, the Watch, the magistrate
- **Rivals:** whichever House is out of favour this season
- **Allies:** the Ridgemere Watch (80 / allied), all five Houses (40 / friendly), the Merchants' Guild (20 / friendly)
- **Offers:** rented rooms in the old city (Known well), a seat at a Council hearing (Sworn)
- **Takes away:** the right to trade within the walls, rentals
- **Missions:** carry ordinances to the estates, count stores at the Hall of Tallies, keep watch at the Quay Gate
- **Visual:** slate blue, a hall under rain

### The Ridgemere Watch
- **Kind:** city (the Watch)
- **Territory:** the Watch House, the Gaol, the wall walk, the quay patrols, the gates
- **Leadership:** the Captain of the Watch
- **Public purpose:** keep the peace in the rain
- **Actual purpose:** keep the peace where the Houses can see it
- **Methods:** patrols, the Gaol, fines
- **Rivals:** the Sump's gangs (unaffiliated), the Syndicate's Ridgemere contacts (−50 / hostile)
- **Allies:** the Council of Houses (85 / allied)
- **Offers:** a member's word heard (Trusted), the Gaol's gate opened early for a friend (Sworn)
- **Takes away:** the benefit of the doubt
- **Missions:** walk a night patrol on the quay, carry a warrant, deliver the Gaol's bread
- **Visual:** dark blue cloaks, a lantern

### House Grayrock
- **Kind:** npc (noble House; quarry and ironworks)
- **Territory:** Grayrock Hall, the quarry, the Ironworks, the Grayrock Road
- **Leadership:** Lord Aldric (always travelling) and Lady Maren (always at home); the heir Tobias at the ironworks
- **Public purpose:** stone and iron for Ridgemere
- **Actual purpose:** the road to Upper Accord, and a voice in the Concord
- **Rivals:** House Fell over timber for the ironworks' charcoal (−15 / tense)
- **Allies:** House Ashcombe (30 / friendly)
- **Offers:** iron goods at the House's price (Trusted), work for a Chapter's members, and a camp on the Grayrock Road
  (Trusted)
- **Missions:** carry ore tallies to the Hall of Tallies, escort Lord Aldric's carts, keep watch at the quarry
- **Visual:** grey and black, a split stone

### House Fell
- **Kind:** npc (noble House; timber)
- **Territory:** Fellhall, the sawmill and millpond, the log yard, the forest
- **Leadership:** Lady Isolde Fell and her son Corwin
- **Rivals:** House Grayrock (−15 / tense)
- **Allies:** House Brinewater, which buys its masts (35 / friendly)
- **Offers:** a camp in the Fell woods (Trusted), timber for building (Sworn)
- **Missions:** bring herbs from the forest edge, carry the mill's accounts, watch the log yard at night
- **Visual:** deep green, a felled pine

### House Ashcombe
- **Kind:** npc (noble House; charcoal and glass)
- **Territory:** Ashcombe House, the Glassworks, the Kilnhouse, Burners' Row
- **Leadership:** Lord Edric Ashcombe, who still blows glass himself
- **Allies:** House Grayrock (30 / friendly)
- **Offers:** glass and lamps (Trusted); a Fire-Gifted wolf's work valued and protected (Sworn)
- **Missions:** carry glass to the quay stores, bring charcoal tallies, keep watch on the kilns
- **Visual:** amber and soot, a lamp

### House Brinewater
- **Kind:** npc (noble House; ships and rope)
- **Territory:** Brinewater House, the Ropewalk, the Brinewater Yard on the quay
- **Leadership:** Lady Petra Brinewater and her consort Leif
- **Allies:** House Fell (35 / friendly), House Vesk (25 / friendly)
- **Offers:** passage by boat (Trusted), rope and rigging for a camp's palisade (Sworn)
- **Missions:** carry rope to the yard, run a message to the Harbourmaster, keep watch on a ship in the yard
- **Visual:** sea green and white, a coiled rope

### House Vesk
- **Kind:** npc (noble House; fish, smoke and salt)
- **Territory:** Vesk Manor, the Salt Store, the smokehouses and Fish Hall on the quay
- **Leadership:** Lord Magnus Vesk
- **Rivals:** the Sump's smugglers of salt (unaffiliated)
- **Allies:** House Brinewater (25 / friendly)
- **Offers:** salt and smoked fish for a camp's stores (Trusted)
- **Missions:** deliver meals to the smokehouses, carry the salt tallies, keep watch on the Salt Store
- **Visual:** salt white and smoke brown, a fish on a hook

## Upper Accord and beyond

### The Concord
- **Kind:** npc (supranational council and religious authority)
- **Territory:** Upper Accord's halls of council and archives; authority, not land, everywhere else
- **Leadership:** a voting council of member states' rulers; Concord Representatives and appointed agents
- **Public purpose:** peace among the member states, the Bright One's order, the regulation of Gifts
- **Actual purpose:** contested; its friends call it order, its enemies (Ash among them) call it control
- **Methods:** votes, envoys, archives, the Warden Order's reach
- **Allies:** the Church of the Iron Saint (70 / allied), the Warden Order (60 / allied), the Crown (10 / neutral)
- **Offers:** papers that open borders (Trusted), and an envoy's word for a Chapter's Hold (Sworn)
- **Takes away:** a Chapter's good name in every member state
- **Missions:** carry sealed letters between cities, guard an envoy's lodging, fetch archive copies
- **Visual:** white and gold, a ring of linked hands

### The Warden Order
- **Kind:** npc (Warden)
- **Territory:** the Warden training grounds in and around Upper Accord, and wherever a Quickened wolf is found
- **Leadership:** the Order's masters, few and old
- **Public purpose:** find, train and protect Quickened wolves, and protect others from them
- **Actual purpose:** the same, at a cost the Quickened don't always choose
- **Methods:** assessment, recruitment, containment, confrontation (setting bible §17)
- **Allies:** the Concord (60 / allied)
- **Rivals:** the Syndicate, which would sell a Quickened wolf (−60 / hostile)
- **Offers:** training for a Gifted member (Trusted), and the Order's protection when a member Quickens (Sworn)
- **Takes away:** its patience with a Chapter hiding a Quickened wolf
- **Missions:** report rumours of strange Gifts, escort a Warden on the road, keep watch at the training grounds
- **Note:** Quickened wolves never travel as party residents (Phase 3). The Order's interest in a Chapter's Quickened
  member is a story for the DM, not a mission.
- **Visual:** undyed grey, a closed eye
